# Persona walkthrough: personalized drinkware

**Date:** 27 October 2026
**Persona:** First-time merchant selling 11 oz and 15 oz mugs/tumblers in matte and gloss finishes, with a front logo and optional back text. One shopper may order four items with one design and two with another.

## Attempted path

Started in the local preview at Home, opened the first-use and no-products examples, followed **Add product** to its Shopify handoff, then returned and followed **First product setup → Configure product → T-shirt** as an explicitly labelled apparel analog. In the T-shirt customer preview, opened **Add your artwork → Choose position → Left chest**. Finally, followed the local sample order **#1046 · Hoodie, two designs** to its production sheet. No sample was renamed, no Shopify product was created, no draft was saved or published, and no real checkout was attempted. This was an agent-simulated local walkthrough, not human research.

## Five useful findings

1. **The demo does not contain drinkware.** Its product list has a T-shirt, hoodie, and polo. In the no-products state, **Add product** leads to a page that says a connected store would open Shopify’s product form and that the local preview creates no product. I stopped there. See [empty catalog](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/02-empty-catalog.png>) and [handoff](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/04-product-handoff.png>).

2. **The first-use guide is short, but the selected examples report misleading progress.** It names three understandable jobs—configure a product, connect the theme, test a customization—without overwhelming detail. In both **No Shopify products** and **First product setup**, it showed **3 of 3 complete** with all three checks selected while the page still invited the merchant to add or configure a product. That is an observed local-preview mismatch; persistent demo guide state may explain it, so it does not establish the clean production onboarding state. Fix the preview’s progress to follow its selected state, and avoid marking a first-use guide complete before the merchant has a product. Tradeoff: state-accurate guidance can re-surface tasks that a merchant has completed outside Insignia. See [expanded guide](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/01-first-use-guide-expanded.png>).

3. **The editor covers artwork placement, methods, sizes, and prices.** The T-shirt analog has named views, multiple placements, image-relative artwork areas, decoration-size scales (explicitly distinct from garment sizes), per-placement methods/prices, and optional customer movement within a defined area. Its customer preview offers position, artwork upload, size, and method, but no text input. “Orders without artwork” defers artwork for the merchant; it is not text personalization. The production contract supports uploaded or deferred artwork, with no free-text field ([contract](../../../packages/contracts/src/v1/pricing-config.ts#L66)). See [editor](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/05-editor-placement.png>) and [customer preview](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/03-customer-artwork.png>).

4. **Variant photo alignment is not a per-capacity print limit.** The UI says **Shared artwork area**, offers **Adjust for Black**, and asks the merchant to confirm alignment. Source shows variant overrides are image-relative placement rectangles; physical millimetres belong to a decoration-size step, and the visualizer says those values are display calibration, not manufacturing precision ([geometry](../../../packages/visualizer/src/geometry.ts#L24), [limits](../../../packages/visualizer/README.md#L9)). The sample worksheet says **Physical size not configured**. These controls do not establish 11 oz versus 15 oz production limits. See [alignment](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/06-photo-alignment.png>) and [worksheet](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/07-production-sheet.png>).

5. **Separate designs have a local order/worksheet path; the customer preview is not checkout.** Sample order #1046 shows two hoodie designs, quantities 2 and 3, and a two-page A4 production sheet. It is marked for testing and says to use original artwork files for production. Storefront source models quantities per variant inside each design group and lets shoppers start a new design or edit a retained one, so 4+2 is representable in the source model. I could not exercise that buyer flow against a connected shop. See [order](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/08-two-design-order.png>) and [worksheet](</Users/pc/Development/Insignia/.artifacts/persona-audit-2026-10-27/drinkware/07-production-sheet.png>).

## Proposed fix and tradeoff

For this merchant, keep the shopper’s designs as separate groups with per-variant quantities, add optional text as a real customer field with clear limits and an order/production output, and model provider print specifications separately from photo alignment. A minimal drinkware setup could collect the manufacturer’s printable width and height per capacity (and only add finish-specific limits if the supplier differs). Tradeoff: explicit specs and text rules add setup work, but prevent a photo rectangle from being mistaken for a production guarantee. The merchant should be able to leave provider dimensions unset when they do not have verified specs, with the production sheet continuing to say so.

## Unsupported or unverified here

- 11 oz/15 oz, matte/gloss variants, mug imagery, and capacity-specific physical print limits: absent from the local catalog and not represented as variant-specific physical dimensions in the current geometry model.
- Optional personalized back text: no customer text control was visible in the preview or represented in the current artwork-choice contract.
- Real Shopify product creation, publication, buyer checkout, provider-specific print-file generation, and fulfillment: not attempted. The sample production sheet is a local test worksheet, not production evidence.

## Primary sources and inference boundary

- **Observed UI:** the local-preview pages and screenshots above. They establish only what those apparel/sample states displayed.
- **Source-confirmed:** the local storefront model’s design groups and per-variant quantity controls ([buyer UI](../../../apps/storefront/src/customizer.tsx#L793), [quantity review](../../../apps/storefront/src/customizer.tsx#L1121)); the production-sheet label uses configured dimensions or “Physical size not configured” ([renderer](../../../apps/web/src/shared/production-sheet-render.ts#L455)).
- **Vendor references:** [Customily’s Text box guide](https://help.customily.com/articles/2111674173) describes customer text input and live preview; [Zakeke’s Print Area guide](https://zakeke.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016879139-How-to-define-a-Print-Area) describes calibrating an image with a real-world ruler distance before setting a print area. These are examples of vendor workflows, not claims about Insignia. The conclusion that this mug scenario needs verified provider dimensions and customer text rules is an inference from the stated product requirements and the observed/source-confirmed gaps.
