# Apparel competitor structure: methods and product setup

First-party help-center review, 22 October 2026. This compares documented information architecture and responsibilities; it does not claim that the vendors' pricing or production models match Insignia.

- **DecoNetwork keeps enabled techniques in a central Decoration Processes area.** Admins enable processes such as DTG, embroidery and screen printing, then use per-process links for defaults like display name, pricing method, production format, file/design types and color palette. Enabling a process makes it available during product setup but does not update existing products automatically. [Decoration Processes Overview](https://help.deconetwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/234946427-Decoration-Processes-Overview)
- **DecoNetwork separates shared pricing defaults from product exceptions.** Decoration Pricing defines default methods and price structures across supported processes; individual products can override those values, and a global pricing-method change can affect products that still inherit the default. Some modes price by placement, area, product color, quantity or process-specific factors. [Decoration Pricing](https://help.deconetwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/236150748-Decoration-Pricing)
- **DecoNetwork treats decoration areas as a product-side compatibility/configuration layer.** Shared area definitions have dimensions and supported-process assignments and can be applied to product categories; changing a shared area updates products using it. On a product, the merchant chooses applicable areas and can configure process prices per area. [Manage Decoration Areas](https://help.deconetwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000381228-Manage-Decoration-Areas), [Set Up Decoration Pricing Per Location](https://help.deconetwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019586573-Set-Up-Decoration-Pricing-Per-Location)
- **InkSoft separates account-level printing capabilities and reusable print-price grids from each blank product's Decoration tab.** The shared Pricing area controls capabilities such as digital/vector printing, maximum screen-print colors and minimums; reusable screen-print grids encode quantity, side and color prices, including multiple named grids. [Pricing Options](https://help.inksoft.com/hc/en-us/articles/46724089991963-Pricing-Options), [Screen Print Pricing](https://help.inksoft.com/hc/en-us/articles/46724059783579-Screen-Print-Pricing)
- **InkSoft product setup selects among those shared methods and grids, then owns product-specific sides and regions.** A blank product can activate account-enabled methods, select its print-price grid, set product-specific production days, palettes and max colors, and define enabled sides/print regions or designer overlays. [Edit Products – Decoration (Blank Products)](https://help.inksoft.com/hc/en-us/articles/46724037237787-Edit-Products-Decoration-Blank-Products)

**Qualified implication for Insignia:** Both apparel-native products give merchants a central place to maintain reusable methods or technique rules, then configure actual product eligibility and print locations in product setup. That supports keeping **Decoration methods** under Settings while making product configuration the authoritative place for method, location, decoration-size and final-price choices. Settings can offer a useful route to product setup or a clearly qualified usage summary, but the sources do not support moving the product list or product assignments into the method library. Their multi-axis global price grids, minimums and process-wide technical restrictions are capabilities of those larger production systems; they should not be imported as requirements for Insignia's final per-product method/location/size prices.

Zakeke supplies a second customization-app comparison. Its documented menu separates Customizable Products, Printing Methods and Pricing Rules. Printing Methods configure reusable customization/output behavior; product setup owns sides, variants and areas. The July menu groups Printing Methods under Customization Experience and Pricing Rules under Commerce Rules. [Printing Methods](https://zakeke.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016836099-Printing-Methods), [July 2026 menu update](https://zakeke.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/28477656423196-Product-Update-July-2026)

Zakeke allows pricing rules on methods or products, but explicitly warns against overlapping assignments: product rules override method rules. This is evidence that global pricing is possible, and that inheritance needs visible precedence. Keeping final product pricing as the MVP's single editing place is an Insignia recommendation, not an industry-wide requirement. [Pricing Rules](https://zakeke.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015984779-Pricing-Rules)

The products shown on Insignia's methods preview are a prototype shortcut for reaching the sample product workflow. They should move to Products in the intended navigation. A generic route to Products can remain; a live usage count should not be shown because current independent copies do not establish reliable source lineage. No application code was changed by this review.
