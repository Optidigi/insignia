# Shopify first-party app inventory for Home research

26 October 2026. Research only. No app code, merchant account, install, permission, theme, or store data changed in this pass.

## Scope and evidence rules

The canonical Shopify roster is the Help Center's [Apps made by Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/apps-by-shopify) page. It currently names 39 entries. The [Shopify App Store partner listing](https://apps.shopify.com/partners/shopify) is a second check for current developer listings, but its displayed total and pagination differed across crawled snapshots. I use the Help Center page for inventory scope, not as proof that every entry is currently installable.

The roster includes products that behave like sales channels, mobile apps, developer tools, and regional services as well as embedded merchant apps. Their advertised purpose does not prove they provide a distinct App Home landing page.

Shopify says apps it makes are usually free, not always free. The constraints column records material plan, region, service, account, or data dependencies visible in the linked first-party source. Where the source reviewed did not establish a price or plan gate, I do not infer one from the app's name or category; check its linked current listing before purchase or install.

Evidence labels mean:

- **Native observed**: a Shopify UI was inspected in the owner-authorized dev store, as recorded in [Home native app observations](home-native-app-observations-2026-10-26.md). This applies only to Flow, Forms, Search & Discovery, and Translate & Adapt.
- **Documented Home/overview**: Shopify Help describes an app Home or Overview page and its content or task behavior. This is evidence about documented purpose, not a fresh visual observation.
- **Documented work areas**: official instructions describe the app's pages or workflows, but do not establish its Home composition.
- **Listing only**: the official catalog or App Store description supports the job-to-be-done. The archetype below is an inference from that job, not a claim about a screen.

App Store promotional images and videos were not used to claim current UI layouts. For the eight additional candidates requested for closer consideration, the sources below distinguish documented Home/Overview behavior from a listing or task manual. No additional app was installed or natively inspected.

## Names, omissions, and what to inspect next

- The Help Center roster uses “Shop Channel,” “Shopify POS,” and “Store Migration.” App Store listings use “Shop,” “Point of Sale,” and “Shopify Store Migration.” Digital Products retains the `/digital-downloads` URL. Treat these as naming differences, not proof of separate apps.
- Stocky remains named on the Apps made by Shopify roster, but its retirement page overrides any assumption that the app is still installable or usable.
- Hydrogen is on the roster but links to Shopify developer documentation, not a regular Shopify App Store app page. Counter links to Shopify's dedicated Counter product. Both are included because the canonical roster includes them.
- Shopify Product Network is included in the transition section because it is first-party but no longer installable. It is not counted among the 39 Help Center roster entries.
- Shopify SimGym is included separately because it is a newer App Store product not yet shown in the Help Center's Apps made by Shopify list. Its documented storefront-home focus is useful for buyer-flow testing, not merchant-admin Home design.
- Shopify Sidekick and Shopify Admin Home are not separate first-party apps in this roster. They belong to the host Admin experience and were already covered by the existing Home research.
- The Help Center app descriptions and official App Store entries establish developer/catalog identity and intended work. They do not prove a distinct app Home, a page's current visual layout, or what Insignia should copy. Only the four native apps named above were inspected for this comparison.

For a follow-up native review, the best additional candidates are Launch Check, Inbox, Digital Products, and Knowledge Base. Collabs and Marketplace Connect are worth a focused look only if a concrete creator or marketplace workflow is in scope. SimGym warrants a separate storefront test if the owner wants to compare actual buyer-home layouts. Bundles, Checkout Blocks, Audiences, and Stocky need no extra Home-reference inspection for Insignia's current product scope.
