# Orders and buyer workflow audit

24 October 2026. Product and workflow review of Orders, artwork handoff, replacement, payment context, buyer price review, and the documented merchant-to-buyer integration boundary. This pass found one actionable workflow issue.

## P2. Surface order readiness in the artwork download flow

**Task and repro.** In the local merchant preview, select or open demo order **#1042**, then choose **Download artwork**. The order detail shows `Paid`, `Artwork complete`, and `Test`. The Orders table displays payment and artwork badges but no Test badge. The download dialog reports files ready and offers **Download ZIP**, without repeating test, payment, cancellation, or production-hold status. The [dialog capture](../../../../.artifacts/admin-popup-review-2026-10-24/orders-settings/desktop/order-artwork-export.png) shows this state. Compare the [production sheet](../../../../.artifacts/admin-popup-review-2026-10-24/orders-settings/desktop/order-production-sheet.png), which labels this as “Test order: this sheet is for testing,” and the [batch sheet review](../../../../.artifacts/admin-popup-review-2026-10-24/orders-settings/desktop/orders-batch-production-sheets.png), which surfaces order-level holds and test status.

**Evidence.** [OrdersOverview.tsx](../../../apps/web/src/islands/OrdersOverview.tsx#L642) renders payment and artwork status but not `isTest`. [PurchaseArtwork.tsx](../../../apps/web/src/islands/PurchaseArtwork.tsx#L533) shows the Test badge on the detail page. [ArtworkArchiveDownload.tsx](../../../apps/web/src/components/ArtworkArchiveDownload.tsx#L173) presents the ready count and order identity; [its download action](../../../apps/web/src/components/ArtworkArchiveDownload.tsx#L291) remains available without an order-readiness summary. The ZIP manifest says “This archive is not production approval” and records payment, test, and cancellation status, but only inside the archive after download ([artwork-archive.ts](../../../apps/web/src/shared/artwork-archive.ts#L200)). The worksheet separately warns for cancellation, payment/refunds, test status, and missing artwork ([production-sheet-render.ts](../../../apps/web/src/shared/production-sheet-render.ts#L77)).

**Impact.** The archive contains current original artwork that a merchant can pass to a production partner. A bulk export can include test or held orders, but its ready-files report does not identify which orders need review. The manifest and worksheet warnings reduce the risk, but the merchant has to open another artifact or already know to check it.

**Smallest fix.** Show a compact readiness summary beside the ZIP action, using the same checks as the production sheet. Name affected orders and give concise reasons such as `Test order`, `Payment/refund review`, `Cancelled`, or `Artwork needed`. For multi-order exports, show counts and identify each affected order. Add a visible Test marker in the Orders index. Keep source-artwork retrieval available for testing, while making the handoff context clear at the download decision.

**Principle.** Shopify’s [layout guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/layout), [index-table pattern](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/patterns/compositions/index-table), [modal guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/latest/web-components/overlays/modal), and [content guidance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/content) informed the review of task focus, row scanning, modal actions, and concise copy. The [Checklist Design table](https://www.checklist.design/design-system/table), [data-table](https://www.checklist.design/web-app/data-table), [modal](https://www.checklist.design/design-system/modal), and [empty-state](https://www.checklist.design/web-app/empty-state) pages informed checks for scannable statuses, clear actions, and distinct empty, no-results, and error states. These sources do not prescribe disabling artwork downloads or define a product-specific hold rule.

**Reviewer judgment.** This action packages artwork a merchant can hand to a production partner. The report should carry the readiness context that already exists in the worksheet and order record. Memorisely’s [visual-hierarchy discussion](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/memorisely_stop-ignoring-visual-hierarchy-in-ui-activity-7349029993810685953-wZK1) and Zander Whitehurst’s [progressive-disclosure discussion](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/zanderwhitehurst_ux-ui-uxdesign-activity-7163115739644698624-uqjX) support placing task-relevant information nearby. Neither defines a hold rule for this product.

**Qualifier.** This is a local preview/demo-fixture finding, not evidence of a live Shopify order or an actual production handoff. The detail page displays the Test badge; the index and download dialog omit it.

## Coverage and limits

I inspected the local Orders index and order detail, plus saved desktop/mobile captures for artwork ZIP preparation, production-sheet preview, and batch production review. Source review covered payment/refund presentation, replacement upload, lock gating, archive generation, and production warnings. Replacement is unavailable after `ARTWORK_LOCKED` or a recorded lock event. The payment panel distinguishes original order total, first fully paid date, refund records, and the need to check settlement in Shopify. I found no separate actionable workflow defect in those states. I also checked the Orders no-results and error-state copy against the empty-state guidance; I found no actionable issue there.

I reviewed the buyer customization and quote-review source and the frozen-draft Customer preview. The price review identifies the complete customized subset and breaks out garment base, decoration, design, and quote totals. The specification defines Shopify discounts, tax, duties, and shipping as separate checkout-owned amounts. I found no concrete buyer-price finding in the inspected flow. The buyer page on port 5173 was a standalone visual prototype, not evidence of live checkout. The documented local Customer preview and merchant configuration do not share a publication transport. Buyer positioning and price configuration are not connected end-to-end to live storefront quotes, Shopify checkout, imported orders, or production output ([customer-placement-v1 note](customer-placement-v1-2026-10-23.md)). This review does not qualify the full merchant-to-buyer-to-order-to-production flow.

All inspection was read-only. I did not publish configuration, alter a demo order, replace artwork, perform checkout, or write to Shopify or a production system. The [gallery follow-up note](admin-gallery-comments-2026-10-24.md) is background for the current modal and worksheet behavior, not a regression finding.
