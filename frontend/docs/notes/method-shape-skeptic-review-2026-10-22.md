# Methods shape: skeptical reconciliation

Read-only product-shape review, 22 October 2026. This reconciles the assignment, pricing, Shopify-pattern and competitor notes against the request for a top-level Methods library, tag-based Smart methods, and a flat-price option. It proposes visible product semantics; it does not claim existing backend support or native Shopify observation.

## Recommendation

Use a searchable **Methods** index and a full-page create/edit **Details** resource with **Method**, **Products**, and **Pricing** sections. Keep one method record per technique; do not create nested pricing-profile records or duplicate method rows for different target groups. A compact side summary should report assignment mode, current match count, readiness and publication separately. Keep a page-level Save Bar.

In **Products**, offer two mutually exclusive modes: **Choose products** and **Match by tags**. Smart rules are in scope because the requested product includes them; treating them as a hypothetical future mode would answer a narrower question. Keep the first rule editor to Shopify product tags with explicit `has any` / `has all` semantics, a live matched count, and a preview. A collection source could be considered as a simple alternative, but do not import collections' mixed product/variant/source/exclusion semantics or build a generic condition builder. Competitors establish that central methods and product-specific setup can coexist, not that their broader rule engines should be copied.

A saved Smart rule remains live: current rule membership follows later tag changes, and new matches become method-eligible without publishing. When a tag change removes a match, show it as no longer eligible for the next product revision; preserve its product configuration and current published offer until the merchant reviews/publishes the product change. Do not represent one-time method copying as Smart matching. Manual mode stays a stable selected product set. Both modes require a per-product list with distinct states: **matched**, **needs setup**, **ready**, **published**, or **out of rule**. Matching is not readiness, and readiness is not customer visibility. Never infer identity from equal method names.

For **Pricing**, the only defensible reusable numeric mode in the reviewed Lean model is **Flat charge per selected location, per garment**. State the unit beside the amount and show a concrete example (`€5 × 2 selected locations = €10 per garment`). The mode applies to every eligible selected product location, independent of decoration size; it is not a one-time per-item or per-order fee. The second choice can be **Set final prices on each product**—a route to product setup, not a shared price matrix. Final method × product location × decoration-size prices remain product-local. A method-wide size table would need a product-local mapping/precedence contract before it could be represented honestly. Missing is unavailable; explicit `€0.00` is free.

A method rate is a shared rule, so saving a changed rate must show an impact review: matched products, products needing setup, product-specific price exceptions, and currently published products. Explain in merchant terms that the new price is used for future product revisions; current published prices and accepted orders stay as they are until the product is republished, and unready matches receive no implied price for locations they have not configured. Do not silently rewrite published offers or turn an unready match into a ready product. If a product has a custom price, identify it as an exception rather than suggesting the shared rate replaced it. Defer once-per-garment fees spanning locations and per-order setup fees; current tuple pricing cannot express them without repeated charges or unsupported checkout behavior.

## Conflicts and implications

- The assignment review recommends manual-only assignment and warns against tag rules; the Shopify-pattern review allows rules conditionally, and the user explicitly requests Smart/tag assignment. This is a scope conflict, not a small UI choice. Choose the simple live tag mode above and define its add/remove effects; do not ship a disabled or save-time-only “Smart” toggle.
- The assignment review limits reusable methods to a name and explanation, while the pricing review adds a shared flat rate and a custom location/size mode. These do not compose as drafted: a reusable per-location rate is coherent, but final product prices need product-local location/size rows. Do not surface “custom prices by location and size” as a second method-wide price matrix. Optional customer-facing labels or artwork constraints belong only where buyer/production behavior is supported.
- The pricing review says method rates seed copied draft rows and later edits do not refresh products; the Shopify-pattern note says changes affect future product drafts/configurations. This hides a critical consequence if the UI also claims Smart methods or “Products using this method.” Choose an explicit shared-rate policy. The recommendation here treats the rate as a live default for future product revisions, with custom product prices shown as exceptions and a pre-save impact review. Published revisions and accepted orders remain snapshots; no automatic publish occurs.
- The assignment and competitor notes warn that independent copies do not prove a live usage relation. If rules/prices are live, persist method identity and rule membership separately from product-specific configuration. Until that relationship exists, show `Matched by rule` and product state, not a guessed usage count.

The index/detail composition and Save Bar follow Shopify 2.0 RC resource patterns in the Shopify review. Public Shopify patterns and competitor help pages are precedents for information architecture, not evidence of Insignia behavior or usability testing. The proposed behavior requires later contract work for live tag membership, stable method identity, price exceptions, and product revision effects; the current name-only catalog should not be treated as a constraint on the UI exploration.
