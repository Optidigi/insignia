# Insignia Settings V1: Shopify ownership and scope

27 October 2026. Research only; no application source, Shopify configuration, theme, or merchant data was changed. This note recommends what belongs in Insignia Settings, what should remain in Shopify Admin, and the product facts the pages can safely state.

## Recommendation

Use four destinations under Settings: Storefront setup, App permissions, Billing, and Customer data requests. Keep Methods as its own top-level app-nav destination, with its existing contextual product-editor entry. This recommendation addresses navigation only; it does not narrow or revise the approved Methods feature scope. These four settings links cover theme setup guidance, app-access diagnosis, app billing status, and a real Shopify-supplied request workflow. Do not add pages that mirror Shopify store settings, staff roles, Shopify subscription tiers, theme editing, or app installation. Do not add a single Insignia enable/disable switch.

Shopify's Settings pattern is for options that configure the app's behavior, grouped by task. It is a UI pattern, not a reason to copy Shopify-owned controls into Insignia. Keep the four destinations as task links; do not repeat the existing Methods app-nav destination inside the Settings body. [Shopify App Home Settings pattern](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/app-home/v2.0/patterns/templates/settings), [Shopify navigation and information architecture](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/navigation)

### Billing scope with the free plus two paid direction

Shopify App Pricing supports a free plan and recurring plans and owns the hosted selection/approval surface. Insignia can link into it and report only the current plan that its trusted subscription/entitlement mapping recognizes. The product direction of Free plus two paid tiers does not supply the tier names, prices, benefits, downgrade behavior, or entitlement map. Until those are decided and connected to server policy, do not display feature matrices, fake limits, recommended-plan badges, or an actionable three-card picker. Do not infer that a zero-dollar development/test subscription is the future commercial Free tier.

The inspected billing adapter currently recognizes one Standard plan and carries a USD 49.99 per 30-day reference price, trial/provider cycle data, discounts and development-term distinction. It cannot identify the new three-tier catalog yet. Treat that as an integration dependency, not a copy change: add exact plan labels, benefit claims, and access behavior only after Shopify plan configuration and server entitlement policy agree. [Shopify plan configuration](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/billing/shopify-app-pricing/plans), [Insignia billing reader](../../../apps/web/src/server/admin/billing.ts), [billing view contract](../../../apps/web/src/shared/billing-view.ts)

### Privacy visibility grounded in the product

The request inbox is justified by a real workflow, but Shopify does not require this particular merchant-facing page. Shopify requires public apps to subscribe to and verify `customers/data_request`, `customers/redact`, and `shop/redact`, acknowledge requests, and complete the required response within 30 days. That is a Shopify App Store developer requirement, not a universal legal deadline for every merchant request; applicable law and merchant decisions remain separate. The current Insignia request row calculates its displayed due date as receipt plus 30 days, so label it as the app's tracked due date and do not imply that opening or downloading a file completes the request. [Shopify privacy law compliance](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/compliance/privacy-law-compliance), [Shopify merchant guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/processing-customer-data-requests)

If V1 needs a short data-and-retention disclosure, show only this source-backed summary near the request workflow (or link to the maintained privacy notice):

- Buyer customization includes uploaded artwork, selected product variants/quantities, method/placement/size choices, and accepted design/economic records. These are customer-associated records even without a separate customer-contact profile.
- Artwork assets have a maximum seven-day retention window. Purchased design/offer payloads have a maximum six-month window and can be erased sooner. A downloaded export does not extend those windows.
- Order IDs, line/economic facts, payment/refund observations, and audit facts are separate from design payload retention; do not tell merchants that all order/customer records disappear after six months. A privacy redaction fences/removes relevant private design/artwork payloads while immutable economic records remain under the existing contract.
- The processor erases contact-bearing raw webhook bodies and captured contact fields after processing. It retains a minimal request receipt and Shopify order links to support the request/export workflow. The JSON export includes order and retained design data; artwork files must be opened from the Shopify order where still retained.

These are product facts from the inspected implementation, not an exhaustive inventory of service telemetry, subprocessors, legal bases, or a conclusion about applicable law. Shopify's compliance guide explicitly says it is not legal advice and directs developers/merchants to address legal obligations separately. Do not publish extra legal claims, promise a specific customer's rights, or claim the data request page fulfils the merchant's legal duties.

## Shopify-owned settings and contextual handoffs

| Setting/action | Owner and V1 handling |
| --- | --- |
| Shopify store subscription, Shopify invoice, staff roles and app access/install/uninstall | Shopify Admin. Insignia may explain an access failure or open Shopify's app management surface; it should not recreate owner/staff roles, app approval, charges, or uninstall. Shopify staff app permissions are explicitly separate from permissions set up through the app. [Staff permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/staff-accounts/staff-permissions/staff-permissions-descriptions), [Uninstalling apps](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/uninstalling-apps) |
| App subscription plan choice | Shopify App Pricing hosted plan selector. Insignia's Billing page is a status/entitlement explanation plus a Shopify link, not a competing picker. Shopify currently documents a billing card on the Shopify app settings page as well. |
| Store currency and store time zone | Shopify Admin > Settings > General. Insignia already uses the shop currency as read-only price context in product setup; do not add a second currency preference or convert prices independently. Shopify app-plan prices are a separate billing domain. [Shopify store details and defaults](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/manage-orgs-and-stores/manage-store-details) |
| Shopify Admin language and regional formatting | Per-user Shopify account preferences, not an Insignia store preference. Shopify states changing account language affects the Admin display, not storefront language. Do not add language/region selectors to Insignia Settings. [Shopify account language and region](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/languages) |
| Storefront language, market currency, theme styling and publication | Shopify Markets and Theme Editor. Insignia's app block and app embed are installed/configured there; Insignia provides the scoped setup handoff and test. Do not create a generic theme or branding settings page. [Theme extension UX](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/online-store/theme-app-extensions/ux), [Theme extension configuration](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/online-store/theme-app-extensions/configuration), [Shopify Markets](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/markets) |
| Merchant support entry point | Use Shopify's native **Get support** action in the embedded app header and Shopify Admin app page, backed by the support contact/channel configured in the app listing. Verify that configuration and destination for the published app. Do not claim that support is unavailable based only on a local preview that lacks the listing context. [Shopify support requirements and surfaces](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/support-your-customers) |

Insignia's current customization app-block schema has a customer-facing `Button label` text setting and an `App proxy prefix`; the cart embed exposes only the proxy prefix. Keep the button's per-block appearance control in the Theme Editor rather than adding a duplicate global branding page. Shopify's theme-setting docs say merchant-entered text settings can be translated through Translate & Adapt. This is different from app-owned extension locale strings: merchants cannot edit those strings, so shipped storefront copy needs Insignia locale files and must not be promised as automatically translated. Treat proxy prefix as integration plumbing, not an ordinary merchant preference; keep it fixed/managed unless a verified supported configuration requires a deliberate advanced control. [Shopify input-setting localization](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/architecture/settings/input-settings), [Theme extension locales](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/online-store/theme-app-extensions/configuration#locales)

## Current state and dependency gaps

- The current `MerchantSettings.tsx` has five rows; the recommended Settings hub has four, because Methods is already a persistent top-level destination and the product editor also has a contextual link. Shopify's navigation guidance says to avoid replicating app-nav content in the app body. This location recommendation does not reduce Methods' feature scope. [Shopify navigation and information architecture](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/navigation), [current app nav](../../../apps/web/src/components/AdminNavigation.astro)
- `MerchantBilling.tsx`, `ReadinessPermissions.tsx`, and `MerchantPrivacy.tsx` match the ownership split. Recent Settings notes confirm that access and privacy pages are not policy editors, plan selectors, or deletion centers.
- Store currency, staff role, and Shopify admin language should remain in Shopify. Product prices are authored in shop currency. Current billing and privacy date renderers use the Shopify Admin/browser locale with a UTC calendar boundary; the privacy endpoint does not currently provide an authenticated shop timezone. Do not add a timezone preference to hide that missing data context. If a local-time date is required, source the real shop timezone through the authenticated integration; otherwise retain the explicit UTC behavior.
- The currently inspected TOML scope manifest is a scope candidate, not a receipt for deployed grants. The permissions page must query live app grants; server authorization and actual staff capability determine access. The candidate's `read_product_listings` optional declaration is currently unused, and order/privacy access is owner-gated in the actual service. Keep those as integration/readiness facts rather than implying that a visible App Bridge scope guarantees access.
- Plan names, prices, benefits, and feature entitlements for Free plus two paid tiers are unresolved. The code recognizes Standard only. Do not describe new tiers in Settings until catalog and server policy are aligned.
- Shopify documents that an embedded app header's **Get support** route uses the support channel configured for the app listing (email is required; a support portal can be selected). The current billing island's fallback text says “Contact support” when it cannot build the Shopify management link, but that local UI state itself does not identify or verify a contact destination. Keep the native support route available and configured; test the published destination before treating that copy as a working recovery action. [Support your customers](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/support-your-customers), [Getting help with apps](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/apps/getting-support)
- Shopify owns uninstall. Its guidance says theme changes can need extra steps, but Insignia uses theme app extensions rather than direct theme-code edits. Verify actual uninstall behavior before telling merchants either that theme setup is automatically removed or that manual cleanup is needed. Shopify's compliance docs separately require the app to process `shop/redact` after uninstall; this remains an app integration responsibility, not an Insignia Settings control.
