# Methods configuration direction

Research and design synthesis, 22 October 2026. The owner rejected the name-only library and requested a fresh operational Methods resource with assignments and pricing. This supersedes the earlier name-only UI recommendation for future design work. It does not change implementation, contracts or live Shopify data.

## Proposed shape

Promote Methods beside Orders and Products. Use a searchable resource index, then the same full-page create/edit editor. Index columns: Method, Pricing, Product scope, Setup. Use concise exact summaries rather than an entire product catalog in each row. Avoid bulk actions without a useful supported operation.

The detail page has General, Pricing and Products sections in the main column, with a concise scope/price/setup summary in the desktop aside. On phones the same sections stack. Use the contextual Save Bar for Save/Discard. Supporting product selectors, confirmation and impact review can use dialogs; the primary editor is a page. No wizard is needed for repeat edits, and an editable index alone would bury conditions and charging semantics.

## Pricing

Use a single-select choice, not a flat-fee on/off toggle:

- Fixed decoration price: one amount in shop currency, explicitly per selected location on each garment. Example: EUR5 on front and back means EUR10 decoration per garment. Zero is free. This is a complete price, not a base amount plus hidden surcharges.
- Product-specific prices: product locations and decoration sizes supply their final prices. The Methods page shows coverage and links into the relevant product pricing context; it does not invent a global matrix of product-local location/size IDs.

Quantity, stitch/color calculations and order setup fees are possible later pricing responsibilities, not extra switches in this first exploration. Product-specific exceptions, if supported, replace the method rate and must be identifiable. Missing prices never become zero.

## Products

Explore two assignment choices: Choose products and Match by tags. Exact manual selection uses Shopify's product picker; the tag mode uses selected existing tags and any/all matching. Do not add a general nested rule builder or a separate collection mode at the same time. A Shopify collection source is a credible alternative if merchants prefer maintaining rules entirely in Shopify; it is not required alongside tags.

The assigned/matched product list belongs in this method's detail page. It needs thumbnails, names, search/pagination when necessary and an understandable setup outcome. Matching does not fabricate garment positions, size compatibility or a valid published offer. Show products needing setup and a direct recovery action.

Saved tag rules remain live and membership follows tag edits. They are not a one-time product import. The proposed boundary is automatic eligibility with explicit product review/publication for buyer-visible configuration changes. A rate/rule edit shows affected products and any custom prices before applying. Product configurations and accepted orders are preserved; never silently republish or erase product work. This is a product decision for the prototype and later integration, not behavior available in the current catalog.

## How the five reviews were weighed

1. Shopify-pattern reviewer: adopt Index to full Details, sections, Save Bar and an aside. Do not add separate internal/customer names by default.
2. Pricing reviewer: adopt two clear modes and per-location charging example. Keep product-local size/location pricing out of a pretend global matrix.
3. Assignment reviewer: retain identity, readiness, selector and collision warnings. Reject manual-only as the UI exploration scope because it does not examine the owner's requested smart assignment. Its copy-into-draft proposal remains evidence of a possible integration path, not the definition of automatic assignment.
4. Merchant desk reviewer: adopt small/large-catalog and two-placement walkthroughs. Reduce the proposed many stage labels to clear scope and setup feedback, with publication detail only when relevant.
5. Skeptical reviewer: adopt real live-tag semantics, explicit global-change impact and separation of product geometry from method policy. Do not carry every proposed lifecycle state into the page.

These are five independent model desk reviews, not merchant interviews or usability-test results. Their detailed reports are linked below.

### Consequences for the Insignia proposal

Use the Discounts editor as the main configuration reference. Its grouped fields, conditional inputs, concise summary and contextual Save/Discard fit method configuration better than a short library modal. Borrow the product picker and the separation between membership controls and resulting products from Collections. Do not copy its whole source/exclusion system into the MVP.

The proposed Choose products / Match by tags choice is deliberately simpler than the current native Collections source model. Shopify does not require those choices to be exclusive; exclusivity is our proposed scope limit to avoid rule/manual/exclusion precedence in the first prototype. If simultaneous rules and manual exceptions prove necessary, add them with explicit inclusion/exclusion semantics later.

Pricing must state what is charged. The native discount checkbox demonstrates why Fixed amount alone is insufficient. The per-location-per-garment rule above is a proposal, not an owner-approved meaning of flat fee. Show a concrete front/back calculation in the prototype and keep the charging unit visible next to the amount. Product-specific pricing is the second proposed mode; percentage discounts and once-per-order discount behavior are not decoration-pricing requirements merely because Shopify offers them.

Keep product geometry, garment images and supported decoration sizes in the product setup. The method page can show assigned products and setup gaps without becoming a second product editor. A product may use several methods. Zero matches, missing prices, long method/product/tag labels, many products, rule changes and cancellation without saving must be included in the subsequent UI checks.
