# Home research: native Shopify app observations

26 October 2026. Observed in Chrome on the owner-authorized `insignia-rewrite-dev` store. These are first-party reference observations, not Insignia acceptance criteria. The owner authorized installing several Shopify-made apps for this research and explicitly reaffirmed the dev-store scope after the Search & Discovery permission review.

## What was installed and changed

Installed **Shopify Search & Discovery, Shopify Flow, Shopify Forms, and Shopify Translate & Adapt**. Each listing identified Shopify as developer and Free pricing. Their installed-app entries or native app routes were observed. Search & Discovery had an explicit final permission review; the other three App Store install actions opened the installed application through Shopify's managed flow.

Created and saved one inline Shopify Forms reference named **Home research — inactive example**, observed as **Inactive**. Its identifier in the overview was `1217346`. This exposed the transition from a first-use empty state to a populated overview. It was not activated, embedded in a theme, attached to a landing page or submitted. No notification or automation was activated. No product, order, stock, theme or translation was deliberately edited. The expanded pass also auto-saved one unconfigured Flow draft, renamed **Home research — inactive example**. It remains **Inactive**, with **Trigger not defined** and **Not recently run**. No trigger or action was configured, and no workflow was turned on or executed. The research form and four installed apps remain available for later inspection.

Shopify Order Printer was considered through its official listing and installation review, but installation was cancelled. Its screenshots are first-party published visual evidence for this comparison, not a live observed Home. Shopify Bundles was considered through official documentation, not installed; its configuration adds little to the Home comparison and an unnecessary bundle/cart-transform integration should not be introduced for research.

## Native comparisons

| Reference | State actually observed | Useful pattern for Insignia | What this does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shopify Admin Home | Store has orders and is not public. Home surfaces an order task as well as launch/payment/domain/shipping suggestions. The Ready to sell disclosure explicitly separates ability to sell from public storefront launch. | Existing operational work can coexist with unfinished setup. A status must name its scope. | Its large Sidekick hero and store-wide analytics are not an app Home requirement. No orders were fulfilled. |
| Search & Discovery | Initial app overview: one optional setup task, manual mark-as-done control, collapsible/dismissible guide, dated search/recommendation panels with zero rates and explicit no-data messages. Collapsed and expanded guide were observed. | Guide collapse preserves room for ongoing work. Metrics have a period and a destination. Setup state and no activity are separate. | A manually checked task does not verify theme correctness. Insignia need not copy many empty metric panels. No filters/recommendations were changed and no task was marked done. |
| Flow, first use | Official app entry rendered Workflows with creation/template/import actions, news and featured templates. The first-use creation prompt appeared before a workflow existed. | Core resource work can be the landing page. An empty account does not need empty operational metrics. | News/templates fit Flow’s broad integration ecosystem; they are not an Insignia requirement. |
| Flow, one inactive draft | After auto-saving an unconfigured draft, the landing became a table with Workflow, Status, Last run, Latest updated, Trigger and Tags. The row showed Inactive, Not recently run, Trigger not defined. | Resource existence, activation, activity and completeness are separate facts. A draft deserves a resume destination, not repeated first-use onboarding. | This draft has no trigger or actions and was never activated. It does not prove a running or successful automation. |
| Flow, activity and detail | Global Recent runs showed its no-runs state and 14-day completed-run retention. The inactive workflow detail had Draft/Version history, preview/Edit and a resource-scoped no-runs explanation. | Global activity and one resource’s history differ. Empty history is not an empty account. | No completed, failed or waiting live run was induced. Those states are documented evidence only. |
| Forms, no forms | One first-use empty state: brief product explanation and Create form. No empty analytics dashboard was shown. | A new merchant needs one useful first action. Do not render a full steady-state dashboard of zeros before there is anything to manage. | The large native illustration is not a reason to add invented Insignia imagery. |
| Forms, one inactive form | Saving the inactive example replaced the first-use empty state with a dated metrics strip and a resource table containing the form, its Inactive badge and zero activity. Create form moved into the host header. | Content changes after the first resource exists, even before it produces activity. Resource existence, publication/activation and performance are distinct states. | One inactive form is not an active business or an order-receiving state. Nothing was published. |
| Forms, Active filter with no matches | Metrics/context remain; the list says No forms found and asks the merchant to change filters/search. Returning to All restores the inactive form. | A filtered empty state should not repeat onboarding or imply the underlying resources vanished. | This is a list-filter precedent, not a recommendation to add Home filters. |
| Translate & Adapt | Default-language context, grouped resource links, secondary language/quick-start information and an informational update banner. No languages translated. | A useful app landing page can be a task/resource hub without analytics. Main work and contextual guidance can have different prominence. | Its many resource categories and full side rail are not appropriate by default for Insignia's smaller scope. No language, market or translation was changed. |

The first Forms empty state was visually inspected before saving the example but was not saved to a file; do not substitute the later filtered-empty capture for it.

The public reference listings are [Search & Discovery](https://apps.shopify.com/search-and-discovery), [Flow](https://apps.shopify.com/flow), [Forms](https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-forms), [Translate & Adapt](https://apps.shopify.com/translate-and-adapt), and [Order Printer](https://apps.shopify.com/shopify-order-printer). Shopify's [Forms management documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage) confirms that new forms are inactive until activated and describes the overview's metrics/table. Its [Search & Discovery analytics documentation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/storefront-search/search-and-discovery-analytics) describes the reports and period.

These observations cover real first-use, resource-present-but-inactive, no-activity and filtered-empty examples. Active-order operations, permission failures, stale data and partial Home failures remain Insignia design scenarios derived from source contracts and general guidance. They were not induced in the native apps and must not be described as observed native states.

No theme, product, order, subscription charge, customer conversation, email or workflow activation changed. The new Flow fixture is the only additional saved research resource.
