Correct checkout enforcement remains a trust premise; a temporal gap cannot be proved absent from current health reads. Undetected context-mismatch or unsigned-required purchases during such a gap remain incident exposure. This path's record describes authorized offer matching, not historical Function execution.

The local implementation must reject the alternative unless trusted composition admits the exact provider-owned creator identity and independently qualifies current Function/artifact enforcement. Names, source labels, token presence and an enabled mode flag alone are insufficient. Missing admission remains a rejection. Production admission stays absent until the creator predicate, existing enforcement/readiness composition and representative native path qualify; local injected provider/admission doubles are not that proof.

The delegated decision authorizes local implementation, meaningful tests, independent review and ordinary integration without another routine principal checkpoint. It grants no deployment, scope/version, Shopify order/test checkout, broker, storage, payment or rollout resource. Existing M8 native-operation permissions must be established before those effects.
