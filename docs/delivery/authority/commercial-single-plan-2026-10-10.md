# Owner decision — one paid commercial plan

Effective 10 October 2026. This records the owner's explicit conversation decisions. It is manual owner authority, not a provider receipt or claim that billing is configured.

The owner first stated that all existing features belong to one paid plan and that no commercial configuration exists yet. After current first-party competitor research, the owner selected exactly:

> USD $49.99 + $0.10 after 100 included

For “demo/trial,” the owner selected:

> 14-day live trial

## Adopted commercial terms

- One paid plan includes all released, in-scope Insignia features. Supported technical capacity, publication/readiness, required-product enforcement and retention remain binding; “all features” does not promise unlimited capacity or unfinished features.
- USD $49.99 subscription per 30-day billing cycle. Include 100 qualifying paid customized orders in each paid cycle; additional qualifying orders cost USD $0.10 each. No usage fee ceiling or multi-store pooling was adopted.
- A qualifying usage unit remains one distinct non-test order first fully paid with at least one verified Insignia customization. Item quantity, products, designs or groups within one order do not create extra units. Plain orders are excluded. Refunds, restocks, later cancellations and repeated notifications do not reverse or duplicate usage.
- 14-day live trial of the same paid plan from authoritative activation, with subscription and usage waived. No retrospective trial billing or separately approved permanent demo tier. Provider trial/contract timestamps remain authoritative; reinstall does not create an invented trial reset.

The owner decisions supersede the earlier three feature-differentiated commercial plans prospectively. Historical provider receipts, contract records, feature-policy snapshots, plan-transition/recovery controls and prior evidence retain their exact meanings. Existing valid quotes and retained historical access are unchanged. Versioned feature authorization remains server-side, including inactive/unrecognized policy denial and compatibility with historical downgrade/plan changes.
